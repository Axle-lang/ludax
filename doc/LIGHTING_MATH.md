# Lighting mathematics

The authoritative formulas behind `doc/LIGHTING.md`. Rule: **a formula enters the engine only with an independent
check** — a deterministic numerical integral in f64 (compensated summation), a closed form derived a second way, or
a conservation law. f32 code is then checked against the f64 code with a stated ULP/relative error budget.

## 1. Radiometry (units fixed once, in `LightUnits`)

- Radiance `L` [W·sr⁻¹·m⁻²], irradiance `E = ∫_Ω L(ω) cosθ dω`, intensity `I` [candela], illuminance [lux].
- Rendering equation at a surface point, outgoing radiance toward `v`:
  `Lo(v) = Le + ∫_Ω fr(l,v) Li(l) (n·l) dl`, `fr = fd + fs`.
- External units follow **glTF `KHR_lights_punctual`**: directional in lux, point/spot in candela. Photometric to
  radiometric factor 683 lm/W kept in one constant; scene values are relative to a reference white (§9).
- Lambert: `fd = ρ/π`. Energy: `∫ fr (n·l) dl ≤ 1` for every `v` (tested, §4).

## 2. Light sources, exactly

- **Point** (radius 0): `E = I (n·l)⁺ / d²`.
- **Sphere** (radius R, distance d, `sinσ = R/d`): irradiance of a uniform sphere of radiance `L` on a differential
  area, `E = L π sin²σ · F(θ,σ)` where `F = cosθ` when the sphere is entirely above the horizon
  (`cosθ ≥ sinσ`), `0` when entirely below, and the horizon-clipped closed form (Snyder 1996) between. Implemented
  in f64 and f32; **verified against a 2-D quadrature of the spherical cap** (error < 1e-6 relative), including the
  clipped regime. A point light with radius is shaded as this sphere — no `1/d²` singularity, no hot spot.
- **Directional with angular diameter** `δ` (disc, angular radius `r = δ/2`): same formula with `σ = r`;
  `E = Ls π sin²r` at normal incidence. Sun: `δ ≈ 0.533°` (`r = 4.65 mrad`), moon `0.52°`. Penumbra widths follow
  from `r`; shadow tests compare to `w = 2 r · d_occluder→receiver` (§6).
- **Spot**: glTF cone, `scale = 1/max(1e-3, cos(inner) − cos(outer))`, `offset = −cos(outer)·scale`,
  `a = sat(cosθ·scale + offset)`, `S = a²` (smooth, monotone, C¹ at the inner edge, 0 at the outer).
- **Finite range window** (artistic cut, applied *on top of* physics): `w(d) = sat(1 − (d/R)⁴)²`; `w(0)=1`,
  `w(R)=0`, `w'(R)=0`. Documented as a deviation from physics, off by default for voxel flood calibration.
- **Area lights** (rect/disc): linearly-transformed cosines (Heitz 2016); LTC tables generated by a tool through
  deterministic fitting and checked against quadrature.
- **Emissive**: surfaces with `Le`; contribute to bloom/probes, optionally to light via the probe/flood layers.

## 3. Specular BRDF (Cook–Torrance, glTF-aligned)

With `α = roughness²`, `h = normalize(l+v)`:

- `D_GGX(h) = α² / (π ((n·h)²(α²−1) + 1)²)`.  **Normalisation:** `∫ D(h)(n·h) dωh = 1`.
- Height-correlated Smith visibility
  `V = 0.5 / ( (n·l)·√((n·v)²(1−α²)+α²) + (n·v)·√((n·l)²(1−α²)+α²) )`  ( `= G2 / (4 (n·l)(n·v))` ).
- Fresnel Schlick `F = F0 + (1−F0)(1−v·h)⁵`; `F0 = lerp(0.04, baseColor, metallic)`.
- `fs = D V F`. **Reciprocity:** `fs(l,v) = fs(v,l)` (tested).
- Diffuse: Lambert, or Burley/Disney retro-reflection (selectable), weighted by `(1−metallic)`.
- **Energy compensation (multiple scattering):** directional albedo `E(μ,α) = ∫ fs (n·l) dl` (F = 1) is tabulated
  by a tool through deterministic quadrature (not Monte Carlo noise), stored as an asset; Kulla–Conty factor
  `1 + F0 (1/E(μ) − 1)` restores the lost energy. Tests: white furnace within 1e-3 for all `(μ,α)` on the grid.
- **Sampling for the reference path tracer:** visible-normal sampling (Heitz 2018). **Consistency test:**
  `∫ pdf = 1` and the estimator of `E(μ,α)` matches the table within its confidence band, with a fixed seed.

## 4. Conservation and symmetry laws (all are tests)

1. Furnace: albedo 1, uniform radiance `Lenv` ⇒ `Lo = Lenv` (diffuse: `∫ (1/π)cosθ = 1`).
2. Directional albedo ≤ 1 for every `(μ, α, F0)`; = 1 − ε for white at α→0.
3. Reciprocity of `fs`, symmetry of `D`, `V` in `(l,v)`.
4. `∫ D (n·h) = 1`; `∫ pdf_vndf = 1`; sampled-vs-analytic mean agree.
5. Fresnel → 1 at grazing, → F0 at normal incidence; monotone in between.
6. Sphere irradiance at `σ→0` equals point-light `I cosθ/d²` with `I = L π R²`.
7. Inverse-square: doubling `d` quarters `E` (point, and sphere for `d ≫ R`).

## 5. Environment and indirect

- **SH9 irradiance** (Ramamoorthi–Hanrahan): `E(n) = Σ Â_l L_lm Y_lm(n)` with `Â0 = π, Â1 = 2π/3, Â2 = π/4`.
  Check: a constant environment `L` gives `E = π L`; a clamped-cosine lobe is reproduced to < 1 % (max error
  quoted). Projection by deterministic quadrature over an equal-area grid.
- **Analytic sky:** single scattering, Rayleigh `β_R(λ) ∝ λ⁻⁴` + Mie (Cornette–Shanks phase, `g≈0.76`) with exact
  optical depth via the **Chapman function** (no ray-march noise), the sun's colour from transmittance. Checks:
  zenith/horizon limits, sunset reddening monotone, energy of the horizon colour bounded.
- **Voxel flood** attenuation law is *calibrated to the physical inverse-square* — see `voxel/light`; the
  calibration error is measured and reported, not asserted.
- **Hemisphere AO / sky access:** cosine-weighted hemisphere sampling (Malley); estimator checked against the
  analytic occlusion of a known configuration (infinite wall at distance `a`, tilted plane).

## 6. Shadows

- Penumbra from a disc source of angular radius `r` with occluder at distance `a` from the receiver edge:
  half-width `a·tan r` (tested on a half-plane occluder: the visibility ramp is the **exact circular-segment
  area** `(θ − sinθ cosθ)/π` of the disc, not a linear guess).
- Depth-map bias is *derived*, never tuned: normal-offset by `texel · √(1 − (n·l)²)` plus a constant of one texel
  at the cascade's resolution; tests: no acne on a plane at 85° incidence, no leak at a contact edge.
- PCF/PCSS kernels are deterministic (Vogel disc, fixed rotation per pixel from an integer hash).

## 7. Numerics

- f32 hot paths; every function has an f64 twin (the maths layer generates it). Budget per function stated in its
  doc comment, e.g. `D_GGX: rel. error ≤ 4 ulp for α ≥ 0.045`; asserted over a deterministic Pcg32 sweep.
- Division guards instead of traps; denominators have documented floors (`α ≥ 0.045`, `n·v ≥ 1e-4`).
- Sums in tests use Kahan/Neumaier summation; quadrature uses Gauss–Legendre (nodes tabulated, order stated) in
  θ and uniform trapezoid in φ (spectrally accurate for periodic integrands).
- No libm in the shading path: `sin/cos/exp/log/pow` are the deterministic polynomial versions of the maths layer,
  with measured accuracy; `pow(x, 5)` is multiplications.

## 8. Colour

Linear Rec.709/sRGB primaries throughout; sRGB OETF/EOTF exact piecewise, round trip error < 1 ulp-class bound;
tone map: a filmic curve that is **C¹ at its knee** (value and derivative continuity checked by finite
differences), monotone, maps 0→0, preserves hue by operating on a luminance-based max-channel scale above the
knee. Wide gamut/HDR output out of scope for v1 but the pipeline stays scene-referred.

## 9. Exposure

`EV100 = log2(N²/t · 100/S)`; saturation-based exposure `H = 78/(S·q) · ...` per ISO 12232 with `q = 0.65`:
`exposure = 1/(1.2 · 2^EV100)`; auto-exposure from a log-luminance histogram with temporal adaptation
(exponential, time-constant in seconds, frame-rate independent: `a = 1 − exp(−dt/τ)`). Tests: +1 EV halves the
output, adaptation is dt-independent when integrated over the same time.
